# Product Vision — Personal Intelligence Infrastructure

## The promise

Everything useful you do should compound.

Operating System converts ordinary activity into six forms of leverage:

1. **Memory** — what happened, when, where and why.
2. **Knowledge** — lessons and reusable understanding extracted from evidence.
3. **Direction** — decisions, priorities, risks and unfinished work.
4. **Content** — credible material based on real work instead of invented posting prompts.
5. **Story** — a documentary timeline of conflict, choices, setbacks and progress.
6. **Opportunity** — repeated needs, reusable components and possible products hiding inside the work.

## Product laws

- **Evidence before inference.** Generated outputs point back to source events.
- **Capture once, reuse many times.** Users should not repeatedly explain the same context to different tools.
- **Agents are specialists.** Each agent has a measurable job and a bounded output contract.
- **Human authority stays intact.** The system can surface, suggest and prepare; important decisions remain visible and reviewable.
- **Cost follows value.** Deterministic extraction handles obvious work. Model inference is reserved for ambiguity, synthesis and high-value generation.
- **History is queryable.** Users can ask what they learned, why a decision was made, which ideas recur, what remains unfinished, and what could become a business.

## Full vision modules

### 1. Ambient Capture
Manual quick capture exists now. Future connectors ingest GitHub, Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Slack, voice notes, photos, browser activity and selected app events. The goal is not surveillance; it is user-controlled evidence capture.

### 2. Living Timeline
A chronological truth layer where every event can be filtered by project, source, type, importance and tags. It is the audit trail beneath all generated intelligence.

### 3. Memory Graph
Projects, people, companies, products, ideas and concepts become entities. Relationships are evidence-linked. This lets the system answer “how did this idea evolve?” instead of merely retrieving similar text.

### 4. Decision Ledger
Important choices store the decision, rationale, evidence, alternatives and later outcome. The system can eventually detect reversals and lessons from decisions that worked or failed.

### 5. Open Loop Engine
Problems, promises, ideas and incomplete work become explicit loops with priority and source context. Chief of Staff uses this to protect attention from forgotten commitments.

### 6. Knowledge Engine
Lessons are distilled into durable principles, playbooks, checklists, FAQs and reusable patterns. Duplicate lessons should merge rather than create endless notes.

### 7. Ghostwriter
Real work is transformed into posts, threads, scripts, articles, changelogs, case studies and course material. Each draft includes source provenance and can move through idea → draft → ready → published.

### 8. Documentary Producer
The Producer records narrative beats: tension, constraint, decision, attempt, setback, breakthrough and payoff. It can assemble weekly/monthly episodes and long-form retrospectives without recreating the history later.

### 9. Opportunity Scout
Looks for repeated internal tooling, recurring problems, unsolved user pain, duplicated components and unusually valuable knowledge. It should distinguish an interesting idea from repeated evidence of demand.

### 10. Chief of Staff
Produces the operating brief: critical open loops, recent wins, risks, decisions waiting for review, content ready to publish and signals worth revisiting.

### 11. Personal Search + Ask
Question-answering over evidence, not generic internet knowledge. Examples: “Why did we choose D1?”, “What keeps blocking deployment?”, “What did I learn in July?”, “Which project components repeat?”

### 12. Reflection Loops
Weekly and monthly synthesis compares intentions with reality: what consumed time, what shipped, what stalled, repeated mistakes, emerging strengths and abandoned bets worth reopening.

### 13. Opportunity-to-Execution Bridge
A surfaced opportunity can become a project with a validation plan, experiments and tracked evidence. This closes the loop between noticing leverage and acting on it.

### 14. Productization / Multi-tenant Future
If sold to others, each user gets isolated data, connector credentials, retention controls and export/delete tools. The core engine stays the same; only tenancy, identity, billing and permissions wrap around it.
